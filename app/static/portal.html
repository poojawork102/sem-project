Admissions Knowledgebase

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about criteria, deadlines, verification, and scholarship eligibility.

What happens after I submit my application?

Immediately upon submission, your application is processed through Northstar's automatic security check to assign a unique reference code and clear duplicate/velocity screening. Next, admissions officers audit your academic transcripts, followed by faculty committee review.

How do merit scholarships work?

All applicants who check the scholarship consideration box are automatically assessed for merit scholarships ranging from 25% to 75% tuition waivers based on Grade 12 scores and personal statement excellence.

What if my application status says "Verification required"?

This indicates that secondary verification (e.g. proof of residency or additional transcript notarization) is required before final evaluation. Our admissions desk will email you instructions on any supplemental materials needed.

Need Assistance With Your Application?

Our dedicated Admissions Advising Office is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST.
